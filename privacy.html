Lead Agents privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026.

Lead Agents is a personal-use app. Its only user is its owner, Andrew Roxby.

  • It accesses Google account data only for its owner, to carry out tasks he asks his software agents to do.
  • The AI services that power those agents process the data only to carry out his requests.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Credentials stay on the owner's computer.

Contact: github.com/andrewroxby
